# My Favorite Movie: Project Hail Mary

## Overview
**Project Hail Mary** is a *science fiction* movie based on the novel by Andy Weir. It follows a science teacher who wakes up alone on a spaceship with no memory of how he got there.

## Main Character
- **Ryland Grace**, a schoolteacher turned astronaut

## What It's About
The story is about a mission to save Earth from a threat that is dimming the sun. As the memories come back, he has to piece together his mission and solve problems using science.

## Why I Like It
1. The story is exciting and full of surprises.
2. It shows how science and teamwork can solve big problems.
3. The friendship at the heart of the story is memorable.
4. It has humor and heart, not just action.

> A story about hope, curiosity, and unlikely friendship.
